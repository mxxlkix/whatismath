Big ideas

Math makes impossible questions feel possible.

⊙

What if time is circular?

In a repeating loop, every event is both a beginning and a return. The universe could be a story that never ends but still keeps changing.

∞

What if infinity is a door?

There is no final number, only bigger spaces, deeper patterns, and endless ways to describe the same mystery.

△

What if chaos is creative?

Small changes can trigger huge outcomes. Order can emerge from noise, and patterns can emerge without ever repeating exactly.

Beyond the classroom

How does professional mathematics work?

Professional mathematicians do more than calculate answers. They turn curious questions into precise definitions, test ideas, and build proofs that other people can inspect, challenge, and use.

01

Ask a precise question

Start with a pattern, problem, or “what if?” and define exactly what you want to understand.

02

Build a model

Translate the situation into objects, assumptions, diagrams, equations, or algorithms.

03

Find evidence

Try examples, run experiments, search for counterexamples, and look for a repeatable structure.

04

Prove and communicate

A proof explains why an idea must work. Clear writing lets the next mathematician build on it.

Paradoxes

The strangest places where math and reality meet.

01

Banach–Tarski

Cut a ball into pieces and rearrange them into two identical balls.

02

Hilbert Hotel

A hotel with infinitely many rooms can still make space for more guests.

03

Monty Hall

Sometimes the odds improve when you switch doors, even when intuition disagrees.

04

Fibonacci

Nature repeats itself in spirals, whorls, and patterns that feel almost alive.

05

Brownian motion

Random steps create motion that looks organized from far enough away.

06

Conflict and balance can coexist in a system that appears stable but changes always.
